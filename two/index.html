Ask Us Anything →
Which Ideal machine is best for a busy café in India?
For high-volume cafés serving 200+ cups daily, our Barista Perfetto and La Bella Black are most recommended — featuring multi-boiler technology, E61 group heads, 9-bar Italian rotary pumps, and PID temperature control for consistent espresso extraction throughout the busiest hours.
Do you provide installation and barista training across India?
Yes. Ideal provides free on-site installation, professional barista training, and a pan-India service network covering all major cities. Our team is available via video calls, on-site visits, and expert guidance. Spare parts are readily available even years after purchase.
What is a bean-to-cup coffee machine?
A bean-to-cup machine grinds fresh coffee beans and brews espresso with a single touch — no barista skills required. Our Ideal D24 Pro is a fully-automatic system producing up to 200 cups per day with IoT smart features, perfect for offices, hotels and quick-service venues.
Are Ideal machines suitable for home kitchens?
Absolutely. The E61 Elegante is designed specifically for home baristas — compact footprint, single-group E61 head, PID real-time temperature control, and a professional steam wand for latte art. It brings café-quality espresso to your kitchen counter.
Do your machines support IoT connectivity?
Select Ideal models feature IoT connectivity — control the machine via smartphone, store personalised brewing preferences, track real-time consumption analytics, and receive automated cleaning and maintenance alerts.
What warranty and after-sales support do you offer?
All Ideal coffee machines come with a standard 12-month comprehensive warranty. Extended warranty and annual maintenance contract (AMC) packages are available. Our after-sales includes 24/7 support, video-call troubleshooting, on-site service, and guaranteed spare-part availability for years.
