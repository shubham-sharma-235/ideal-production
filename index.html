03 — The Craft

Eight quiet obsessions behind every shot.

01

Dual Pressure Gauges

Real-time monitoring of steam & extraction pressures, ensuring consistent output across the entire service.

02

PID Temperature Control

Sensitive transmissions and intelligent adjustments — precise heating, stable temperatures, repeatable cups.

03

9-Bar Italian Rotary Pump

Maintains stable pressure for continuous extraction. Minimal noise, long lifespan, unmistakable crema.

04

Thermal Stability

Large steam boilers paired with complex brew boilers for unwavering thermal performance, even at peak rush.

05

4-Hole Steam Wand

High-curved, dry, powerful steam — the kind that lets a barista pour rosetta after rosetta without compromise.

06

E61 Group Heads

The legendary group — renowned for thermal stability and consistent water flow for speciality coffee.

07

Stainless Steel Boilers

Built to last. Premium SS internals retain heat efficiently and protect the cup from metallic notes.

08

IoT Connectivity

Control via smartphone, track consumption, store brew presets per user, get automated cleaning reminders.

08 — Enquiries

Quietly answered.

A few honest answers to the questions our clients ask most. Need more? Our experts are a message away.

Ask the studio
Which Ideal machine is best for a busy café in India?
For high-volume cafés serving 200+ cups daily, the Barista Perfetto or La Bella Black are our most recommended models — multi-boiler, E61, 9-bar rotary, built for service.
Do you provide installation and training across India?
Yes. On-site installation, hands-on barista training, and a pan-India service network — including readily-available spares years after purchase.
Are Ideal machines suitable for home kitchens?
Absolutely. The E61 Elegante brings barista-grade espresso into the home with a beautifully compact footprint.
Do your machines support IoT connectivity?
Select models do. Smartphone control, brew profiles per user, consumption analytics, and automated cleaning & descaling reminders.
What is the warranty period?
Standard 12-month warranty across the range. Extended warranty packages are available on request.
